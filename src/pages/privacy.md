## Overview

Privacy that mirrors the care we give your volunteers. NexoLink collects only the essentials to verify hours, streamline approvals, and empower communities. Everything else stays yours.

## Snapshot

- Data minimalism: We capture just enough details to verify hours, match opportunities, and celebrate achievements.
- Volunteer control: Download, update, or request deletion of your history anytime.
- Trusted infrastructure: Firebase-backed storage, layered encryption, and continuous monitoring.
- Clear data journeys: Each record has a documented purpose, retention window, and ownership path.

## Information We Collect

NexoLink collects the minimum information required to track volunteer participation:

- Volunteer name, email, and the organization they serve
- Dates, locations, and hours contributed for each activity
- Event or program identifiers when an opportunity is tied to a partner

No biometric, financial, or passive location tracking data is collected.

## How We Use Your Data

Your information powers the experience you expect:

- Display accurate volunteer history and earned achievements
- Generate approval workflows for nonprofit partners
- Create exportable reports for schools, employers, or personal records

We never sell or share personal details with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

## Security and Retention

We implement layered controls to keep every record safe:

- Encryption at rest and in transit on reputable cloud providers
- Role-based access controls across the admin dashboard
- Audit logging for approvals, edits, and exports

Volunteer records remain while your organization is active. You can request deletion at any time.

## Your Choices

Browse without an account or sign in to unlock:

- Manual and QR code hour logging
- Instant access to your volunteer transcript
- Realtime status on pending approvals

Questions or corrections? Reach out and we will respond within two business days.

## Billing

We provide a 14-day free trial to allow you to explore and get a feel for our services. After a transaction is completed, we do not offer refunds.

## Updates to This Policy

When we change how data is used or add new features, we announce updates in-app and refresh the effective date here. We encourage every volunteer and admin to revisit this page regularly to stay informed.

## Compliance

- GDPR-ready: Data requests receive responses within the mandated window, with exports formatted for portability.
- FERPA-aware: Logs and transcripts can be shared with educational institutions while honoring student privacy.
- Quarterly audits: Internal reviews keep access controls, retention, and encryption aligned with best practices.
