Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 3, 2025

NexoLink values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application, NexoLink. By using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

Information Collection and Use

We collect minimal information solely to track users’ volunteer hours. This includes basic details such as the volunteering site, hours logged, and the dates of participation. No sensitive personal data is collected or shared.

Browsing Without an Account

You can browse the app and access general information without creating an account. Account creation is only necessary for logging and tracking your volunteer hours.

Data Security

Your data is securely stored and used exclusively for tracking your volunteer activities. We implement reasonable security measures to ensure the integrity and confidentiality of your information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
